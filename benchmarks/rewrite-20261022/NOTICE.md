# MCTS text attribution

The text in `answers.jsonl`, `preference-grades.jsonl`, and `preference-scores.json` contains model-generated rewrites or quoted passages based on the MCTS test set. The rewrites were generated on October 21–22, 2026 and are not human reference answers from the MCTS authors.

Source: [blcuicall/mcts](https://github.com/blcuicall/mcts/tree/0cae0aa45381b738c00a18af68e2c36156382160), revision `0cae0aa45381b738c00a18af68e2c36156382160`. Original sentence locations and hashes are listed in `cases.json`; upstream file hashes and URLs are in `sources.json`.
